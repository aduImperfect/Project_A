CAMERA-WINDOW PLAYGROUND

Based on the "camera-window" technique described in Itay Keren's Scroll Back (Jump Bug, Rastan Saga, Street Fighter, Fez). The dashed inner rectangle is the camera window. The screen only scrolls once the player's collider (blue box) touches a window edge — and it moves in that same direction.
