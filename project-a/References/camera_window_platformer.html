CAMERA-WINDOW PLAYGROUND — SIDE-SCROLLER EDITION

Horizontal axis keeps the classic camera-window push (Rastan Saga / Street Fighter). The vertical axis is now gravity-driven and uses platform-snapping (Super Mario World / Rayman): the camera stays vertically locked while the player is airborne or standing still, and only re-aligns — smoothly, at an adjustable speed — the moment the player lands on a platform at a different height than the one it last snapped to. A vertical safety window (top/bottom insets) still nudges the camera during big jumps or falls so the collider never leaves the screen mid-air.
